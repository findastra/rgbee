# rgbee draft artwork

A pet app by Astra. Generated with the built-in image tool, using the Cage family style, by OpenAI Codex (GPT-6), 2026-10-11, America/Denver. Status: draft, awaiting owner review.

## Generation prompt

Create a 3x3 grid sprite sheet of the SAME original pixel-art desktop pet shown exactly nine times, one complete character per evenly spaced square cell. VERY FINE crisp pixel art with small pixels, polished SNES-era detail, about 80 art pixels tall per sprite. Flat solid colors with a few discrete shade blocks, crisp hard pixel edges, no anti-aliasing, no gradients, no dithering, no 3D. A solid thin BLACK pixel outline traces every silhouette. Tiny dot eyes with one white highlight, small pink blush cheeks, cute and friendly. Same body, same proportions, same palette, same size and centered pose in every cell; ONLY facial expression changes. Row-major mood order: neutral calm, eyes closed/blink, happy smile; curious, worried, sad; unwell, shocked, asleep. Curious uses a raised brow and little open mouth; eyes remain symmetric and face forward. Every sprite fully visible with generous transparent padding, no clipping, no overlaps. Actual transparent alpha background, not black/white/gray or checkerboard. No grid lines, borders, letters, numbers, words, logos, shadows or watermarks. Do not draw question marks, exclamation marks, Z, tears, sweat or bandages; the app composites mood symbols later.

Character specification: RGBee is a fuzzy, round cute bee with bright NEON RED, GREEN, and BLUE stripes across a dark charcoal body. Clearly visible short fuzzy edge but crisp pixels, cyan-violet wings held symmetrically to each side, two curved antennae with tiny WHITE glowing-light-bulb tips (discrete white pixels, no halo). Tiny feet. Face is friendly and readable. Preserve an unmistakable bee shape. No yellow/black traditional bee palette; red-green-blue stripes are its identity.

## Deliverables and limits

`sheet-20261011.png` is the preserved generated transparent sheet. The nine dated PNG frames are 256 by 256, sliced with shared scale and original alpha using `scripts/build-desktop-art-20261011.mjs` from the desktop pet controller repository. `idle-20261011.png` is the draft app icon. Mood order is idle, blink, happy, curious, worried, sad, sick, alarmed, sleep. The manifest records the paths. These drafts do not change the app, website, runtime, registry, or account integrations. Artwork remains a draft until Astra approves it.
